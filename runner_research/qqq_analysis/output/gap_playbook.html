The Gap-Down Bounce

When QQQ opens well below yesterday's close, the day's biggest up-leg usually starts in the first half hour and tops out early afternoon. One rule-set, measured end to end, costs included. Every number is one in-sample year — see the red box before risking a dollar.

1 · SETUP (premarket)

Gap ≤ −0.35× yesterday's range

Overnight gap (open vs yesterday's RTH close) at or below −0.35× yesterday's high−low. Median qualifying gap -0.82%. Fires ~4×/month (47/247 days). No other filter earned its place: below-PDL vs inside-range, prior day up vs down — all flat.

2 · ENTRY

Buy the 10:00 close. Don't wait for green.

The bounce low is set by 10:00 on 63.8% of event days. Demanding price back above the open first costs edge: still-red-at-10:00 entries made +23.2 bps median vs +10.7 for already-green (n=19/28). Weakness is the product.

3 · STOP

−1.0% from entry, hard

Winning days' MAE below the entry: P75 = 0.54%, P90 = 0.74%. Losers run to 1.29%+. A 1% stop catches only the dumps (6/47 days stopped) and caps the worst day at −100 bps (was −131 unstopped). Tighter kills it: 0.5% stops out 20/47 and flips H2 negative.

4 · EXIT

13:30, no exceptions

The up-leg tops out at median 13:23 on event days. Exit ladder (median net bps): 11:00 → +1.8 · 12:00 → +13.2 · 13:30 → +20.2 · close → +12.3. Holding to the close gives back half the move.

If the OR30 system also fires — prefer it

On event days where the OR30 break→pullback system triggered, its trades ran +29.5 bps median at 82% hit (n=11, 10 long) — better entry, tighter structure. But it only fires on 11/47 event days; this time-rule playbook covers the rest. Never run both on the same day (one QQQ day-position max).

⚠ READ BEFORE RISKING MONEY

1 · All in-sample. The event definition, entry, stop and exit were all found on this same 2025-26 year. This is a hypothesis sheet, not a proven system.

2 · It rhymes with the regime. QQQ drifted +32% this year; "buy weakness" is what such a year pays by construction. The 2022 bear year is the acid test — this playbook goes live only if the edge survives there (HANDOFF §6, item 1 + 5a).

3 · n = 47. Forty-seven events. One bad regime-month can erase a quarter's expectancy. Risk sizing: treat like the R-system — ≤1% equity risk per trade against the 1% stop.

4 · Costs modelled at 1 tick/side. Slippage on a gap-down open can be worse; the 10:00 entry (not the open) is partly there to dodge the worst spread window.
