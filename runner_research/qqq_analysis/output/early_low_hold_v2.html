⚠ The "P20 / take-profit" trap, in one line: the give-back exit and the fixed profit target both raised the win rate (up to 49%) — and both lost money vs letting it run. This strategy's entire edge is a fat right tail of a few big afternoon trends; any rule that caps or protects gains amputates that tail. Trailing stops (breakeven, chandelier-ATR) and early time-exits all failed the same way. The only thing worth improving was getting in cheaper — everything else, the market already does better than a rule.
