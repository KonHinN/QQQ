S0 · THE TARGET

"Hit 1.6%" is the recent regime's P80

  • Runner metric: the day's maximum up-leg — from any low to any later high (1-min bars).
  • Full-year P80 = 1.41%, but the mid-year vol regime shift splits it: H1 1.14% vs H2 1.6% — your 1.6% is exactly H2's P80.
  • A fixed 1.6% bar tags only 13.8% of days across the year and would quietly select the loud half.
  • So the study's target is the trailing 60-day P80 (causal, no look-ahead) — "a big day by recent standards". Fixed 1.6% kept as a robustness column.
S1 · THE HYPOTHESIS BATTERY

Nine precursors, one survivor

  • Bars: change in runner probability (pp vs 27% base) for each feature known before the open. n = 217 days after causal warm-up.
  • 3 consecutive up closes is the only Bonferroni survivor (m=10): runner rate 8.9% vs 27.2%, p = 0.0039, same sign in both halves — a negative signal.
  • Wide days collapse too (8.9% vs 25.3%): streaks predict contraction, echoing Phase 1's "volatility clusters, direction doesn't".
  • Everything else fails the battery at Bonferroni level. The details that matter are on the next slides.

S1 · YOUR HYPOTHESES, SCORED

Two dead, one alive-but-small

"Big up candle t-1 → runs today" — REFUTED

Runner rate 22.7% vs 27.2% base (n=22); trend-day rate 4.5% vs 17.5%. And two big up candles in a row happened once all year — big days don't chain, they exhaust.

"Thrust then pause → runs" — ALIVE, n=11

Big up candle at t-2 + small body at t-1: runner rate 36.4% (lift 1.34), trend-day rate 36.4% vs 17.5% (lift 2.08), same sign in both halves. p = 0.1106, CI includes zero. The right shape of idea — needs the OOS years to reach a verdict.

"Gap up is good" — INVERTED

Any gap up: runner rate 20.8% vs 27.2% base. Big gap up (>0.2× prior range): ~16%. The "too much gap is bad" half of your hypothesis was right — but so is moderate gap up. The runners live on the gap-down side.

"Econ releases → runs" — VOL ONLY

FOMC days (n=7): more range, no up-runs. NFP-rule days: wide-day rate 55.6% vs 25.3% (p=0.0517) but direction ~nothing. News buys volatility, not direction. (Flags approximate — verified calendar is a Phase-2 item.)

S2 · THE GAP LADDER

Runners are born gapping down

  • Overnight gap scaled by yesterday's range, quintiles. Runner rate falls monotonically from 36% (big gap down) to 16% (big gap up).
  • Same-sign in both halves: Q1 = 31.6% / 48.0% vs base, Q5 = 5.3% / 12.0% (H1 / H2).
  • Mechanically sensible: a gap-down hands the day a cheap low to run from; a gap-up has already spent the move.
  • Regime warning: this was a +32% drift year — "buy the gap-down" is what such a year rewards by construction. #1 OOS candidate (2022 bear year is the acid test).

T1 · ANATOMY OF THE BOUNCE

The low is set in the first 30 minutes

  • Big gap-down days (gap ≤ −0.35× prior range): n = 47, median gap -0.82%.
  • The up-leg's low is set at median minute 17 (09:47); 63.8% by 10:00, 74.5% by 10:30.
  • The leg tops out at median minute 233 (13:23) — the bounce is a morning entry, an afternoon exit.
  • 57.4% close above the open, but only 38.3% tag yesterday's close — trade the bounce, don't demand the gap fill.
T4 · THE MEDIAN DAY

Two different sessions after the bell

  • Median intraday path (% from open, 1-min): gap-down days grind up and away from the first half hour; other days are flat noise.
  • The divergence starts right where the leg-lows cluster (09:30–10:00) and never mean-reverts intraday.
  • This is the S2 gap ladder made visible — same data, no new claim.
T2 · CAN A CLOCK TRADE IT?

Net of costs, the drift is real but left-tailed

  • Pre-registered time rules on event days, 1 tick/side costs, vs the same rule on all other days (control).
  • open→close: median +16.1 bps (control +4.8), hit 57%. open→10:59: +17.8 bps.
  • 10:00→close is the most consistent: +12.3 bps median, H1 +10.7 / H2 +18.4 — the only rule healthy in both halves on both legs.
  • Means sit well below medians (+10.4 vs +16.1) — some gap-downs keep falling. Naked time-entries need a stop; n = 47.

T3 · DOES THE SYSTEM ALREADY OWN THIS?

Gap-down days are the OR30 system's best days

On event days

11 system trades (10 long): median +29.5 bps, mean +32.3, hit rate 82%.

On all other days

50 trades: median +8.4 bps, mean +5.0, hit rate 58%.

Interpretation

The break→pullback mechanics already harvest the bounce when it triggers — but it only triggers on 11/47 event days. A gap-down size-up or standalone entry is a natural Phase-2 pre-registered test.

n = 11

Eleven trades. This cell cannot be distinguished from luck on one year. Do not size up on this evidence.

STATUS

What we'd take to Phase 2

1 · Gap-down → runner (S2/T)

Strongest, most coherent finding: monotone ladder, both halves, visible in the median path, tradable with a clock. But it rhymes perfectly with a drift-year artifact. Verdict belongs to 2022–2024 data.

2 · Streak contraction (S1)

The only Bonferroni survivor: after 3 up closes, big legs and wide days both collapse. Use as a filter candidate (skip runner-hunting after streaks), not a trade.

3 · Thrust → pause (S1)

Your best original hypothesis. Lift ~2× on trend days, both halves, n=11. Pre-register and wait for OOS.

4 · Dead

Big-candle continuation (inverted), gap-up momentum (inverted), econ-day direction (vol only). Same lesson as Phase 1: yesterday's direction tells you almost nothing about today's.
